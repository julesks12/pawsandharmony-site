Paws & Harmony — Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

Paws & Harmony is designed to respect your privacy. The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.

Data stored on your device

Your pet profiles, daily check-ins, milestones, and settings are stored only on your device using the app's local storage. This information never leaves your device and is never sent to us or to any third party. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

No accounts, no tracking

The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking. The coaching guidance in the app is provided from content built into the app.

Reminders

The app's Reminders feature is a simple in-app list. The app does not send device notifications and no reminder data is processed on any server.
