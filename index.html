About Paws & Harmony

Paws & Harmony is a daily companion for one specific, often stressful stretch of pet ownership: the introduction period, when a new animal joins a home that already has pets. Instead of advice scattered across forums and guesswork, it gives that period a calm, steady structure.

It's made for adopters bringing a new dog or cat home to resident pets — and for foster caregivers, whose introductions happen again and again, sometimes with just a few weeks to get each one right.

Each day follows a light rhythm. In the morning, the app shows where you are in the introduction and what today's focus is. In the evening, a two-minute check-in asks how it actually went: a stress rating from 1 to 5, any incidents worth noting — hissing, barking, chasing, hiding — and how many minutes the pets calmly shared the same space. Calm days build into streaks, and milestones mark the moments worth celebrating: the first relaxed meal side by side, the first nap in the same room.

The Coach follows the staged approach behavior professionals recommend, always at the pets' pace: scent introduction first, then visual contact, then supervised interaction, and finally extended togetherness. Its guidance adjusts to the stage you're in and how your recent check-ins are trending — no rushing, and no shame in stepping back a stage when the pets ask for it.

Paws & Harmony is made by an independent developer who lives with both cats and a dog — and built it after learning firsthand that introductions go better with a plan.

Guidance, not training

Paws & Harmony is not a training program. It doesn't teach commands, and it doesn't replace a certified trainer, an animal behaviorist, or a veterinarian. What it offers is guidance and tracking: a calm daily structure, stage-by-stage suggestions, and a clear record of how the introduction is going — so you know what to do each day, and when it's time to ask a professional for help.

If a pet shows aggression or you're worried about safety, contact a veterinarian or a certified behaviorist.

For shelters & rescues

The hardest part of an adoption often starts after the paperwork — a new pet meeting the pets already at home. Paws & Harmony gives adopters a calm, structured first month, which supports the match you worked to make. The app is completely free, needs no account or sign-up, and keeps all data on the adopter's own phone — so it's easy to recommend and there's nothing for your team to administer.

One thing we're clear about with adopters: it's guidance and tracking for adopters — not a training program — and it points people to professionals when things get hard.

Where does my data live?

All of your data stays on your device — there are no accounts and nothing to sign into. Deleting the app removes its data.

Do the reminders send notifications?

Reminders in the app are a simple in-app list to support your routine — the app does not send device notifications.
