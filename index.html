How it works

A simple daily rhythm for the first weeks together — the period that sets the tone for a multi-pet home.

1

Set up pet profiles

Add each of your pets — the new arrival and the residents — in a couple of minutes.

2

Daily 2-minute check-ins

Log stress level, any incidents, and time spent together in shared space. Quick enough to do every day.

3

Calm streaks & milestones

Watch calm days build into streaks, and celebrate the milestones as your pets settle in.

4

Stage-by-stage coaching

Practical guidance matched to each stage of the introduction, built into the app — no searching forums at midnight.

Works for any pairing: cat-to-cat, dog-to-dog, or the classic dog-meets-cat. Every pair follows the same calm daily rhythm.

For shelters & rescues

The hardest part of an adoption often starts after the paperwork — a new pet meeting the pets already at home. Paws & Harmony gives adopters a calm, structured first month, which supports the match you worked to make. The app is completely free, needs no account or sign-up, and keeps all data on the adopter's own phone — so it's easy to recommend and there's nothing for your team to administer.

Where does my data live?

All of your data stays on your device — there are no accounts and nothing to sign into. Deleting the app removes its data.

Do the reminders send notifications?

Reminders in the app are a simple in-app list to support your routine — the app does not send device notifications.
